# Compact homepage story

## Four moments

- 1936–1937: hikers from Chepelare turn a former border post into a mountain refuge.
- 1947: the hut is rebuilt after the fire in 1946.
- 1964: the larger hut opens on 28 October.
- 2019: renovation and remodelling continue through this year. This does not describe a reopening in 2019.

Each milestone has one short paragraph. The lengthy date comparisons, 1977 historic-capacity panel, source disclosure and repeated introductory paragraphs are removed from the public homepage. The former large Then & now block is consolidated into the same story. Its navigation anchor remains available at the changing photo panel.

## Scrolling and photographs

Natural page scrolling selects the year and corresponding photograph. No wheel, touch or page-scroll event is intercepted or prevented. The photo panel stays alongside the story on desktop and stays in view during the short sequence on phones. Selecting a year updates the picture directly; Enter and Space operate the enhanced controls. The timeline text always remains visible.

With reduced motion enabled, automatic photo switching and animation stop; manual year selection remains available. Without JavaScript, the four short paragraphs and the original photograph are visible, and the year links open real full-size photo files. The page remains compact and usable.

The 1964 milestone is illustrated with the supplied 1984 photograph, explicitly captioned 1984. The 2019 milestone uses the supplied current view captioned Studenets today; it does not assign 2019 as the photograph’s date. The archival postcard remains explicitly undated. Original watermarks, borders, credit strips and captions are preserved.

The archive strip shows three cards side by side on desktop, with horizontal swipe/keyboard scrolling on phones. Each image opens in the viewer. Shared appearances of the same photograph are deduplicated, so the viewer contains six distinct images and returns focus to the actual link used to open it.

New style and script URLs are versioned under heritage-v171 so existing visitors receive the new behavior. The original legacy gallery/menu script remains unchanged for the v170 booking page.

Fifteen active English/Bulgarian phrases cover the new brief copy and controls. Hut names, contact details, rooms, logo, booking URL and private guest data behavior are retained.
