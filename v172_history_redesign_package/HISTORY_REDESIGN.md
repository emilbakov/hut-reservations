# Homepage history

- One compact section: a short introduction, two photographs and three milestones.
- Bulgarian headline: “Преди курорта. В сърцето му и днес.”
- 1936–1937: the former border post becomes the first hut through the work of Chepelare's hikers.
- 1964: the larger hut opens on 28 October.
- From 2021: modernisation begins, as supplied by the owner. No completion date, specific improvements or dated photograph is inferred.
- The 1977 guidebook block, the historical-notes disclosure and the earlier 2019 milestone are absent from the rendered homepage.
- The original hut and the later building are clearly distinguished. Archive photographs are not presented as being taken on the milestone dates.
- Static side-by-side photographs replace the automatic changing-photo history. There is no sticky panel or automatic switching.
- On narrow screens, photographs remain alongside each other and milestones form short rows underneath. The remaining four archive images use a swipeable strip on mobile and a compact grid on larger screens.
- Six unique original photographs remain available through the photo viewer. Full image proportions, archive marks, captions and existing credits are retained.
- All history content is visible without JavaScript. The existing viewer supports keyboard navigation, captions and returning focus to the opener.
- Bulgarian and English copy are included. Fonts remain self-hosted.

The homepage uses `heritage_history_v172.html` and `heritage-v172/history.css`. The legacy `heritage_history_v169.html` delegates to the new include. Previously shipped assets remain on disk for compatibility but the changing-photo script and its stylesheet are no longer loaded by the homepage.
